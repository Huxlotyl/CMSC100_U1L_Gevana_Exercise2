My Projects

EquipSched

EquipSched is a scheduling and resource allocation system designed to help small health centers borrow medical equipment in San Pablo City, Laguna. The project addresses the challenge of limited resources in rural and community clinics by optimizing borrowing schedules and distribution, applying graph coloring and the knapsack problem.

- Created with Java -

- Presented and won 2nd place in the SPARK11 2024 Competition -

- Collaborated with Johanna Paulene Campos, Laura Mae Guerrero, and Kharisa Mae Pasion -
